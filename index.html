Copy costs

Reaching in-game stars 7–12 costs 5 copies per upgrade. Reaching stars 13–18 costs 7 each. Assumes one usable Wish Box provides one chosen copy. Results exclude trials, food, other critters, and box eligibility.

Checks: 4 silver and 1/5 progress = 51 boxes; 3 silver and 0/5 progress = 57 boxes.
